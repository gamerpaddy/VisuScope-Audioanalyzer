ASIO, beat markers, drawn phase, more control.

**ASIO**
- Every ASIO driver installed on the machine is offered for all inputs and outputs: the analysis input, the timeline recording, the generator, the monitor output and the nodes of the routing graph (shown as "name (ASIO)"). No ASIO SDK and no extra library: the drivers are loaded directly. One driver runs at a time (an ASIO limit); input, recording, generator and monitor can share it. All channels of the driver are available, the rate and the buffer size are the ones the driver uses. A driver whose device is not connected is left out of the lists.

**Routing graph**
- Settings with two entries (on / off, normal / inverted, mute ...) can be controlled by a button, key or MIDI note (right-click the dropdown; toggle or momentary, inverted) and by a signal: a control dot at the left edge takes a wire, with a threshold, a hysteresis and an inverted option
- The Phase shift node has a *drawn curve* mode: draw the phase per frequency like a frequency response; a flat curve, and 0 degrees in the other mode, are plain pass-through without delay
- The output device node no longer has the "Separate L / R" input; a merge node is in the right-click menu of a wire
- The search window keeps its size, and the groups no longer wrap

**Timeline**
- Beat markers: right-click a track, *Beatmarkers* finds the beat of the music and marks it on the waveform (with the tempo); hold Shift while selecting to snap to it. Off by default
- Drag the edges of a selection (gizmos at the top and bottom); *Selection to new track*; Alt turns snapping off; dropping audio files on the window adds them as tracks again
- Dragging the playhead in the ruler while playing no longer repeats the same few milliseconds; a selection made during a loop takes the playhead with it

**Scope**: *Intensity* grading, like a digital phosphor scope: the traces of many triggers are added up, a pixel is brighter the more often the signal passes through it (fade and gain adjustable)

**Other**: the stereo window fits its plots (with the frequency numbers) into the window
