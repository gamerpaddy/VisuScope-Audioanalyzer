Impedance measurement, DATS V3 support, Thiele-Small parameters, spectral editing in the timeline.

**Impedance**
- New *Impedance* tool (Measure group): smooth logarithmic sweep through a series resistor, plotted in ohms with phase, with open / known resistor / short calibration of the leads, saved curves to compare and overlay, report block
- DATS V3 is detected when plugged in and set up with its known gain, resistor and channels (including the built-in 1 kΩ calibration resistor)
- Thiele-Small parameters like the DATS: Re, Fs, Qms, Qes, Qts, Le, Zmax from the free-air curve; V(as) and M(ms) by test box, added mass, specified SPL or specified Mmd; also Cms, Bl, Rms, efficiency, sensitivity

**Timeline**
- Spectral editor, time stretch, takes and comping, punch recording, crossfades, beat markers

**Scope**
- Colours for the channels and a blended colour where they overlap, much longer roll history

**Graph and other**
- Stereo tremolo and wow/flutter, drawn phase curve without stutter, better resampler (no hard cut near 19 kHz or aliasing above 30 kHz), the probe mutes only its own output
- Windows tile keeping their relative positions, screenshot button removed
