The routing graph (node editor), profiler and a round of hardening.

**Routing graph (optional, Options > Routing graph)**
- Every input and output becomes a node: input devices with trim and delay, several generators, the timeline, output devices and the analyser
- Effects in between: equalizer (up to 12 bands, draw mode), filters, compressor, limiter, gate, delay, stereo width, channel utility, mixer, split / merge nodes (one channel per port)
- Space opens a node search; wires can be split, inserted into and are loop-proof; named routings can be saved and loaded
- New effects: distortion, fuzz, bit crusher, jitter / wow and flutter, tremolo
- The timeline records from graph nodes and plays into them

**Profiler**
- Timer latency, audio callback timing and Windows DPC / interrupt / CPU counters, with a list of hiccups and their likely cause

**Timeline**
- Overlapping clips crossfade, tracks can be merged or mixed into one, the cursor is set in the ruler, clips must be selected before they are moved

**Robustness and CPU**
- Damaged audio, project, routing and settings files give an error message instead of a crash (fuzz tested); node parameters are limited to their ranges
- The stereo window is about four times cheaper, fewer wake-ups while idle or minimised
- A safety buffer option that holds back audio to hide short stalls of the system
