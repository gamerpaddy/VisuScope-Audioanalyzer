A better harmonics node.

- The harmonics node now makes a real series of even harmonics (2nd, 4th, 6th ...) and / or odd ones (3rd, 5th, 7th ...). Before, it made a single harmonic and the third was about 50 dB down even at 100 %
- Both terms are relative to the level of the signal, so loud and quiet signals get the same amount; at 100 % the harmonics add up to about half the signal level
- A new *Richness* setting decides how far up the series reaches
