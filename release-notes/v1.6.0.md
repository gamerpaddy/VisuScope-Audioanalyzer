The Assistant, and a second round of bug fixes.

**Assistant (button at the top right)**
- Type what you want ("measure the frequency response, impulse response and distortion with a sweep on input mic1") and it sets up the input, the windows and their layout, the sweep and generator settings, and can start the measurements one after another
- Understands English, German, French and Spanish keywords and small typos; example buttons for common tasks; it always shows its plan first. It works offline and is not an AI model

**Fixes**
- Null test: alignment no longer fails on signals with a DC offset, finer delay search, stale data and playback that could not be stopped
- The output reserve (delay) learns per device without provoking gaps, and seeking in the timeline no longer counts as a hiccup
- Convolution node status showed 0.00 s; *Listen A-B* could stay on unseen; restoring a snapshot deleted layouts saved later; held keys added duplicate markers
- The top bar no longer loses its right end in narrow windows
