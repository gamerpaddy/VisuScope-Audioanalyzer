Listening probe, channel splitting, separate-channel outputs and interface size.

**Node editor**
- **Probe** (button or P): hover over a node or one of its dots to hear the signal there
- Right-click a line or an output dot: *Split here into left and right channels* to route the channels one by one
- Output nodes take a stereo pair or each channel separately
- Deleting an effect joins what fed it to what it fed
- The mouse wheel only zooms (pinch on a touchpad works too); long lines can be selected anywhere along their length; the convolution node fits its width with long file names

**Timeline**
- Clicking in the waveform places the cursor, the upper bar of a track selects; the guide line shows only over a waveform
- A *Clip* button hides the red marking of samples above full scale

**Interface**
- Interface size in Options (and Ctrl + plus / minus) for high-resolution screens
- The *file is too large* message now states the limit (300 million samples, about 52 minutes of stereo at 48 kHz)
