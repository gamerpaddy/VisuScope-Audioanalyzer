Node editor undo, no more side bar, and many editor improvements.

**No side bar**
- The input device, channels and monitor are in an **Input** menu at the left end of the top bar (the routing graph takes them over when it is on)
- The settings that were in the side bar are a **Settings** drop-down at the top of the window they belong to: analysis, traces and microphone calibration in Spectrum, spectrogram settings, 3D view, distortion and voltage calibration in Measurements

**Node editor**
- Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo of the patch (a drag is one step)
- Deleting a node joins its neighbours, also for mixers and splits with one input in use
- *Split here into two stereo lines* (a node with two copies of the signal); the channel split is not offered on lines that are mono already
- The add-node menu shows the groups side by side; menus with a name field (Routings, Layouts, Snapshots) stay open while you type
- A *Timeline track* sink node that can add an armed track to the timeline for itself

**Other**
- Jitter / wow and flutter: depth up to 50 ms, rates in steps of 0.1 Hz
- Timeline: option to import new audio at the start instead of at the cursor; the track header faders no longer run under the level meters
