A frequency shifter node for listening to ultrasound and infrasound.

**New node: Frequency shifter** (routing graph, Effect group)
- Moves every frequency of the signal by the same number of Hz (single sideband, no mirror image): down by 30 kHz turns a 40 kHz bat call into a 10 kHz chirp, up by 200 Hz turns 20 Hz infrasound into an audible 220 Hz
- Direction (down / up), shift from 1 Hz to 96 kHz, mix with the unshifted signal
- *Quality*: *Low delay* (accurate above about 400 Hz, a few milliseconds of delay) or *Deep* (accurate down to about 20 Hz, a delay of 0.2 s at 48 kHz); the node shows its delay
- A filter (on by default) removes what would fall below 0 Hz when shifting down or above half the sample rate when shifting up, instead of folding it into the sound
- Ultrasound needs a high sample rate: use an input at 96 kHz or 192 kHz (the routing graph follows the first input device)
